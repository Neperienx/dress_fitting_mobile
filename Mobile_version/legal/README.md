# Legal Documents

This folder contains draft legal documents for the Bridal Studio pilot:

- `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`
- `TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md`

Before publishing or submitting the app to app stores:

1. Replace every `[INSERT ...]` placeholder with the official operator name, contact email/URL, country/region, governing law, and venue.
2. Publish the Privacy Policy at a public, non-geofenced, non-editable URL for App Store Connect and Google Play.
3. Publish an external account deletion request URL for Google Play if account creation remains available in the app.
4. Have counsel review the documents for your launch countries and business model.
5. Keep App Store privacy labels and Google Play Data Safety answers consistent with the Privacy Policy.
