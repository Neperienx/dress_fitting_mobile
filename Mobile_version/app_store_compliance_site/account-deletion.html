Data rights

Request account deletion

Last updated July 8, 2026

You can request deletion of your Bridal Studio user account from inside the app, where available, or by contacting support. This page provides the external account deletion path for app-store requirements.

Send deletion requests to: [INSERT SUPPORT EMAIL]

Subject line: Bridal Studio account deletion request

What to include

  • The account username or login email you want deleted.
  • Your store name, if you are connected to a store.
  • Whether you are requesting only account deletion or also asking about store deletion.
  • A way for us to verify that you are the account holder or an authorized store owner.

Account deletion vs. store deletion

Deleting a user account does not automatically delete a store, inventory profiles, inventory images, tags, or shared session data. This protects a store from accidental loss if one user deletes their account, leaves the team, or created the account by mistake.

Store deletion is a separate workflow and must be requested by an authorized store owner. We may need to verify ownership before deleting shared store content.

What may be deleted or retained

  • Your user account may be deleted, deactivated, or disassociated from store membership.
  • Store data may remain if it belongs to a shared store or another authorized owner needs continued access.
  • Some records may be retained where needed for legal, security, fraud-prevention, audit, dispute-resolution, backup, or operational reasons.
  • Local data on your device may remain until you sign out, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or the device operating system removes cached files.

Timing

We aim to acknowledge deletion requests within 7 business days during the pilot. Completion timing may depend on verification, store ownership, legal obligations, and backup retention.
