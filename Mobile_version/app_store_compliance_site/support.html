What we can help with

  • Pilot onboarding and account creation approval.
  • Store creation, store joining, invite codes, and role access.
  • Inventory photo upload, image sync, tagging, and local image cache issues.
  • Styling session workflows and app usage questions.
  • Account deletion, store deletion, privacy, or legal questions.
  • Bug reports from TestFlight, Google Play testing, or app-store review.

Response time

During the pilot, we aim to respond within 7 business days. Urgent access or data deletion requests should include "Urgent" in the subject line.

Information to include

  • Your account username or login email, if applicable.
  • Your store name, if applicable.
  • Your device type and operating system version.
  • A short description of what happened and the screen where it happened.
  • Screenshots only if they do not expose private customer data or confidential inventory details.
