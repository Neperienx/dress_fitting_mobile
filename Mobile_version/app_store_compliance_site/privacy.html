Scope

This policy applies to the Bridal Studio mobile app, backend services, pilot onboarding, support communications, and related public pages. The app is designed for bridal boutiques, engagement ring stores, and authorized team members. It is not intended for children.

Information we collect

Account information

We may collect usernames, email-like login identifiers, authentication identifiers, account status, account deletion request status, and related support notes. Password credentials are handled by Supabase Auth and are not visible to us in plain text.

Store and team information

We may collect store name, store type, optional location details, user roles, invite codes, join requests, approval status, and account labels for users requesting access.

Inventory content

We may collect dress or ring profile names, optional price information, uploaded inventory photos, image URLs and storage paths, tags or attributes, timestamps, and identifiers needed to sync, cache, update, or delete inventory records.

You are responsible for making sure you have the rights and permissions needed to upload photos or other content. Avoid uploading personal images of customers, staff, or other people unless you have appropriate permission.

Session and styling information

We may collect or store session queues, selected inventory items, likes, dislikes, shortlist decisions, ranking outputs, bride or customer names if manually entered, feedback, reactions, comments, and local session history.

Device and local app data

The app stores some data locally to make the app faster and usable between loads. This may include authentication session tokens, selected store ID, cached inventory metadata, cached image URLs and downloaded image files, local tag selections, session history, shortlist history, and temporary files used for image upload or sharing.

Photos and file access

The app may ask for access to your photo library or files so you can select inventory images. The app uses this access only for images you choose. It does not scan your full photo library or upload photos you did not select.

Support communications

If you contact us, we may collect your name, email address, store name, issue description, and diagnostic context you choose to share.

How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, and manage accounts.
  • Create stores and manage store membership.
  • Generate and approve invite or join workflows.
  • Upload, display, sync, cache, and manage inventory profiles.
  • Allow team members to run sessions and view store inventory.
  • Troubleshoot bugs, support onboarding, and improve reliability.
  • Prevent unauthorized access, misuse, or policy violations.
  • Comply with legal, security, and app-store obligations.

We do not sell personal information, use inventory content for advertising, or use customer or boutique inventory content to train AI models.

AI and debug features

The app has included a debug-only OpenAI inventory image generation feature during development. This feature is gated for development builds and is not intended for production release. The production app should not expose this debug feature or bundle an OpenAI API key.

If AI generation later becomes a public feature, this policy should be updated before release to explain the feature, data flow, provider, retention, user controls, and consent requirements.

How we share information

We use Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and related backend services. We may use Expo and related development or build tooling to build, test, and distribute app versions. If debug AI generation is enabled in development, OpenAI may process prompts and generated image requests for that limited purpose.

Store inventory, tags, session data, and related content may be visible to authorized users who are members of the same store according to their role. We may disclose information if required by law or if reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or prevent misuse.

Security

We use safeguards appropriate for a pilot-stage app, including Supabase authentication, row-level security, role-based access controls, HTTPS/TLS transport where supported, environment-based production configuration, gitignored local environment files, and production gating for debug-only AI generation.

No system is completely secure. You are responsible for using strong passwords and limiting invite-code sharing.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate the pilot, provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and support store continuity. Account records are retained while accounts are active. Store records, inventory profiles, images, tags, roles, and session data are retained while the store remains active.

Deleting a user account does not automatically delete the store or its inventory. Store deletion is a separate workflow and should be requested separately by an authorized store owner.

Account deletion and data deletion

You can request account deletion from inside the app where available or through the account deletion page. We may need to verify your identity and your authority to act for a store before fulfilling requests.
